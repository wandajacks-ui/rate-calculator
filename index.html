Common Questions

How do I know if I’m undercharging?
If your income doesn’t match your goals or workload, you’re likely undercharging. Use the calculator above to compare your current rate to your target income.

What hourly rate should I charge?
Your rate should cover your expenses, income goals, and available working hours. The calculator helps you estimate a realistic number.

How do I price a project?
Estimate your hours, multiply by your hourly rate, and add a buffer. The project calculator above does this automatically.
